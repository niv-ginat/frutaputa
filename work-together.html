Work

together With
Papaya

Commissions & collaborations. Murals, painted tiles and one-off pieces. Based in Madrid, happy to travel.

I paint murals, tiles and one-off pieces for cafés, bars, shops, homes and brands that want a little attitude on the wall. Every piece is designed for its space and painted by hand.

01 — What

Murals

Interior and exterior walls, shopfronts and windows.

02 — What

Painted tiles

Hand-painted panels for kitchens, bars, entrances and street corners.

03 — What

One-off pieces

Paintings, objects and gifts made for one person or one place.

04 — What

Collabs

Limited drops, prints and merch with brands, venues and other artists.

01 — How

Say hello

Tell me about the space, the idea, the timing and roughly what you want to spend.

02 — How

Sketch

I visit or look at photos and come back with a sketch and a fixed quote.

03 — How

Paint

A deposit books the dates. Then I paint, usually over a few days to a few weeks.

04 — How

Show it off

I photograph and film the finished piece, and we both share it.
